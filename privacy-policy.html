1. Overview

Robloxsplay is a browser-based games website operated by [CLIENT LEGAL ENTITY] ("Robloxsplay", "we", "us"). This policy explains how information is handled when you visit [FINAL DOMAIN] and play the games.

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. You do not need an account to browse or play. The current build does not include login, payment, user-generated profiles, analytics scripts, advertising scripts or a server-side database.

2. Scores stored on your device

Some games save your best score in your browser's localStorage. This is a small piece of data kept locally on your device. It is not transmitted to Robloxsplay and is not used to identify you.

  • Stored examples: a best score, a best move count or a best reaction average.
  • Purpose: to show your personal best the next time you play that game.
  • Control: clearing your browser's site data removes these scores.
  • Personal data collected by this feature: none.

3. Contact messages

If a contact address is published, you may choose to email us. We will receive your email address, the content of your message and any details you include. We use that information only to respond and to keep a record of the conversation where necessary.

Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information. The static contact form in this build prepares a draft; it does not transmit data to a server. The client must supply a monitored contact address and confirm the lawful basis for handling correspondence before launch.

Contact details: [MONITORED CONTACT EMAIL].

4. Third-party games and embedded content

This build embeds nine games from MadKidGames — Cooking Love Chef Restaurant, Subway Surfers, Pepi Hospital Learn and Care, Toca Boca Life Neighborhood, World Cricket Championship WCC Lite, It Girl Story - Celebrity Life, Free Kick Football 3D Soccer, Offroad Outlaws and Hot Wheels Race Off — using the provider’s official iframe code. When the embed loads, MadKidGames and its advertising or technology partners may set cookies or process technical data under their own privacy policies. Robloxsplay does not host or modify the game and does not add advertising code to this player. The game pages identify the provider and link to the official MadKidGames pages and fallbacks.

No other third-party game embeds are currently loaded by this site. Review the MadKidGames privacy policy and cookie controls before using the embedded player; provider consent or cookie choices may apply.
